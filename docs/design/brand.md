# Brand & design direction

Sources: the official logo (`public/brand/logo.png`) and the client's homepage references
(`reference-home-a.png`, `reference-home-b.png` in this folder).

## Colour tokens (sampled from the logo)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `plum-950` | `#2A0833` | darkest hero/footer backgrounds |
| `plum-900` | `#3C0C48` | primary brand colour: headings, nav bar, dark sections |
| `plum-800` | `#4E1052` | gradients, hover states |
| `plum-100` | `#F3ECF6` | soft section backgrounds, icon chips |
| `gold-500` | `#F0A824` | primary CTA buttons, accents, underlines |
| `gold-400` | `#F4C04A` | highlighted headline words, hover |
| `gold-100` | `#FCE4B4` | soft accents |
| `ink` | `#1E1626` | body text on light |
| `white` | `#FFFFFF` | |

These supersede the navy `#10243C` from the planning chats. Contrast rules: gold buttons use `ink`
text (not white), and white text appears only on `plum-800` or darker.

## Typography
- Display headings: a classic serif that echoes the logo wordmark (Playfair Display), with the gold
  italic highlight seen in reference A ("*Solutions Partner*").
- UI and body: Inter.

## Layout patterns taken from the references
- Dark plum hero with a lighthouse photo/illustration, a headline with gold-highlighted words, primary gold
  CTA + outline CTA, and an icon row (Hire Smarter · Operate Compliantly · Manage Efficiently · Grow Sustainably).
- Stats bar (4 figures) between the hero and the content. **Hidden until the figures are confirmed.**
- Service ecosystem: 4 group cards around a circular "End-to-End People Solutions" graphic.
- "Why choose" grid of 6 icon features, then an Industries image-card strip on dark plum.
- Live-demo panel with a laptop mock-up. **Hidden until a real demo exists.**
- Header: logo + strapline, menu, outline "Request a Demo" + gold "Contact Us →".

## Decisions
- Process steps stay **Discover → Plan → Execute → Review** (from the company presentation), not the
  reference's "Understand → Plan → Execute → Deliver".
- The four strategic pillars (Brand, Marketing, HR, Strategy) stay on Home and About. The 13 services
  are grouped by the reference's four **service groups**.
